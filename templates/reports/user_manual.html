1. System Overview & Dashboard

Decore Builders is a secure, cloud-hosted construction management portal built specifically to optimize civil contractor and builder workflows.

Dashboard Visual Panels:

  • KPI Analytics Cards: Tracks overall active worksites, total registered crew size, budgets/revenues, total expenses, and aggregate net profits in real-time.
  • Actionable Alerts Box: Flags low material stock balances (below 5 units), pending client receivables milestones, unprocessed weekly payrolls, and pending expense approvals directly as soon as you log in.

2. Worksites Management

Each construction project site has its own dynamic financial sheet, worker logs, and material inventory balances.

Step-by-Step Site Setup Workflow:

Step 1
Navigate to the Worksites page and click the Add Worksite button in the top right.
Step 2
Fill in the site name, location, start date, and client details. Input the agreed project valuation (Total Budget) and initial site setup costs.
Step 3
Assign a supervisor from the registered engineer list to give them management access to this site. Click Save.

B. Live Financial Analytics:

Within a worksite's detail view, the system computes Live Budget Utilization (Spend vs Profit Margin) and Client Receivables (Amount Paid vs Balance Due) displayed in beautiful, animated doughnut charts. If a site is brand new, clean grey fallback visual states are displayed automatically to prevent system crashes.

3. Workforce & Crew Directory

Keep a complete register of all masons, laborers, electricians, and supervisors.

A. Registering Employees:

Register workers under the Employees tab with their role, wage rate, residential address, and phone. Upload a photo and use the interactive cropping tool to crop it into a neat square. You can also upload Aadhaar cards or ID images directly.

Note: The app checks for duplicates on mobile phone numbers automatically to prevent double registrations.

4. Daily Attendance Logging

Attendance logs serve as the mathematical base for calculating employee payroll.

Daily Sheet Management:

Select a calendar date on the Attendance sheet and assign statuses in bulk. Status rules are structured as:

  • Present: Entitles the worker to 100% of their daily wage.
  • Late: Truncates wage calculation by 50% for the day.
  • Overtime: Grants 150% of their daily wage rate.
  • Absent: Entitles the worker to 0.0 wage.

Database Query Optimization: Bulk attendance sheets pre-fetch active workers in a single operation, keeping page transitions lightning-fast.

5. Materials Procurement & On-site Inventory

Decore Builders tracks live stock balances by subtracting logged consumption from logged deliveries on each site.

Step-by-Step Delivery Logging Workflow:

Step 1
Go to the Materials tab and click Log Delivery. Select the worksite, supplier, and delivery date.
Step 2
Select materials from the dropdown. Standard price and unit will auto-populate from the Master Catalog. Type the quantity.
Step 3
Click + Add Row if logging multiple materials. Total voucher cost calculates in real-time. Click Save.

B. On-Site Live Balance Table:

Live balances are calculated as Delivered quantity minus Used quantity. The stock count automatically turns Red if the balance falls below 5 units (low-stock warning), and remains Green if healthy. Quantities support fractional decimal inputs (like 4.5 bags or 12.8 tons).

C. Logging Material Stock Usage (The Gear Icon):

Click the gear icon next to any material row to record used stock. The backend checks current balances in real-time, blocking entries that exceed current stock to prevent negative balances.

6. Operational Site Expenses

Track cash outgoings like machinery rentals, fuel, safety gear, and permits.

Expense Logging:

Log non-wage expenditures like fuel bills, machinery rentals, or permit costs. Upload receipts and assign approval statuses. Approved expenses are automatically added to the site's total spend calculations.

7. Weekly Payroll & WhatsApp Receipts

Never calculate payroll manually. Decore Builders generates weekly wages automatically.

Weekly Salary Flowchart:

Step 1
Open the Salaries page. Select the weekly period (Ending Saturday) to auto-compile all wages based on attendance.
Step 2
Review and input any custom **Bonus** or **Deductions** for workers. Click **Finalize Week Salaries**.
Step 3
Click **Pay**, log the transaction reference, and click the **WhatsApp** icon to send the secure receipt link.

B. WhatsApp Signed PDF Receipts:

The system builds a WhatsApp link with a secure, cryptographically signed token. This allows workers to open and download their official PDF receipt directly on their mobile phones without logging in, while preventing access to others' details.

8. Executive PDF Reports

Create print-ready reports in the Reports tab:

  • Workforce Directory Report: Roster of all workers, wage rates, and contact details.
  • Worksites Ledger Report: Project valuations, budgets, spendings, client billing balances, and progress percentages.
  • Financial Ledger Audit: Chronological log of payroll distributions, payment receipts, and operational expenses.
  • Materials Inventory Report: Raw material deliveries, unit valuations, and supplier logs.
  • Executive Summary: A high-level audit metric PDF summarizing workforce, active worksites, and aggregate ledger totals.

9. Technical Maintenance & FAQs

FAQs:

  • Supabase DB Backups: Backups are automated and managed directly in your Supabase database console.
  • Render Free Tier Sleep: If using the free tier, the server enters sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity. Simply refresh the page and wait 30-50 seconds for it to wake up.
  • Role Restrictions: Site supervisors can only view and edit logs for worksites explicitly assigned to them. Administrators retain full access across all modules.
